FINAL SELECTED MODEL - CHEXPERT PROJECT

Model:
ConvNeXt-Tiny variants ensemble

Components:
1. ConvNeXt-Tiny baseline
2. ConvNeXt-Tiny regularized fine-tuning
3. ConvNeXt-Tiny with Focal Loss
4. ConvNeXt-Tiny with Label Smoothing Regularization for uncertainty labels

Dataset:
Kaggle CheXpert-v1.0-small

Task:
Multi-label classification of frontal chest X-rays.

Labels:
- Atelectasis
- Cardiomegaly
- Consolidation
- Edema
- Pleural Effusion

Split:
- Train: 80,008 frontal images
- Validation: 10,017 frontal images
- Test: 10,000 frontal images
- Patient overlap: 0
